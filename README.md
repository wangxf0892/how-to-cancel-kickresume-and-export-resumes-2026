## Part 1: How to Cancel Kickresume Auto-Renewal Step-by-Step

To make sure your credit card or PayPal is not charged for the next billing cycle:

1. Log into your account at **kickresume.com**.
2. Click on your **Account Avatar / Profile Icon** in the top navigation bar.
3. Select **Account Settings** -> navigate to the **Subscription & Billing** tab.
4. Locate your active plan and click **Cancel Subscription** (or "Turn off Auto-Renew").
5. Follow the confirmation prompt until the status displays **"Cancelled - Plan will expire at the end of the current billing period"**.
6. **Take a screenshot** of this page for documentation in case of auto-deduction disputes.

⚠️ **Critical Warning**: Once your paid plan expires, Kickresume restricts PDF downloads and locks multiple resume versions to basic templates. Export all documents to PDF/DOCX immediately.
